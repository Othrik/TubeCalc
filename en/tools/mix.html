Mix Calculator

Reaction / stock recipes: enter "stock → final concentration" and the amount is computed automatically (V = Cfinal × Vtotal ÷ Cstock), with ×-folds, % v/v, % w/v and powder weighing supported; leave concentrations blank and type amounts directly for manual mode. Water is filled in automatically and your data is saved in this browser.

ComponentStock / MWFinal conc.Amount
Fill any combination you like: Volume mode — enter stock concentration + final concentration (same-dimension units) and the amount is computed automatically; Weighing mode — pick g/mol in the stock column and use mol/L for the final concentration, or pick a mass unit such as % w/v or mg/mL directly (no MW needed) and the amount column gives a mass; Manual — leave concentrations blank and enter a volume (μL) or mass (mg/μg/g) directly. Common cases: ×-fold buffers (pick × as the stock and the final-concentration unit locks to ×; 5×→1× takes Vtotal/5); pure liquids like ethanol (final conc. as % v/v, blank stock = 100%, so 70% takes 0.7×Vtotal; diluting from 95% stock — set the stock to % v/v 95 as well); agarose/SDS etc. (final conc. as % w/v, 1% = 10 g/L). Changing concentrations recomputes automatically; when both stock and final concentration are set (or a mass-concentration unit is used), the amount number is computed and locked — only the unit can be changed; clear the final concentration to enter an amount manually. Powder/mass amounts occupy no volume (dissolve, then bring to volume): recipes involving molecular weight/weighing show "make up to total volume", all others show the solvent volume (= total volume − sum of liquid amounts).
